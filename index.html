Festival Plaza is a dice game in a park you can walk around. Six trucks, six dice, and only two prime parking spots. Reach 20 Fame, or be the last truck still open.

Before the gate opens

The park is already turning.

The Ferris wheel and the coaster are up while you choose a truck. One or two local players. One or two parking spots. Classic is the dice alone. Signature skills, the mode in these pictures, turns on each truck’s own ability. Rivals take the trucks you do not.

The six trucks

Pick a window. The crowd picks back.

Signature skills are on for the Festival in these frames. Classic turns the abilities off and leaves the dice. Either way, the trucks start as names on the setup screen and models on the path.

The roll

Hold what you like. Serve the rest.

Three of a number score that number in Fame, and each extra matching die adds one. Separate sets stack. A heart restores one Reputation. A coin is a Tip. A person needs no set: each one takes a Reputation from an eligible rival. Parking is the other way to be famous. An empty prime spot pays 2 Fame every turn you keep it.

Fame

Numbers

Three alike score their face value. Each extra alike adds 1 Fame. Separate sets stack.

Park

Two spots

P1 and P2 are empty at the start of this round. Keeping one is worth 2 Fame a turn.

Crowd

People

Each people die takes 1 Reputation from an eligible rival. No matching set required.
